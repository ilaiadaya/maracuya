SKEDADDLE

Support

Skedaddle is an arrow escape puzzle for iPhone and Android, published by Maracuya Labs.

Frequently asked

How do I play?

Tap a block and it slides out in its arrow’s direction if the way is clear. Clear the whole board.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game works fully without internet.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and it can’t be restored.

Why do I see ads?

Some versions show ads to keep the game free. Full-screen ads only appear between levels or games, never while you play, and videos for hints or extra moves are always your choice.
