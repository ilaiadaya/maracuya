HEXBLOOM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Hexbloom (“the app”) is a hexa sort puzzle published by Maracuya Labs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

We don’t ask for or store any account, name, email or contact details. Your progress, scores and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

Versions of the app without ads collect no data at all. Versions with ads show them through third-party ad networks (Google AdMob and/or AppLovin). To show and measure ads, these networks may collect:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking in Apple’s prompt)
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • Information about the ads you see and tap

If you’re in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice from the game’s settings. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and AppLovin’s privacy policy.

Children

The app isn’t directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we don’t store personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by ad networks you can use their own tools, such as My Ad Center. Deleting the app removes all game data on your device.

Changes

We’ll update this page if anything changes and change the date above.
