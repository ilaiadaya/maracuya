YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

Updated September 23, 2026.

Who receives your enquiry

Maracuya Labs, operated by Ilai Bachrach, receives the information you submit through this website. For questions about your data or to request deletion, email ilaibachrach@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

When you submit an enquiry, we save your name, email, company, selected services, company size, approximate budget and any notes you provide. We use this information to respond to you, prepare an introductory conversation and discuss a potential project. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

We also save campaign labels included in the page link, such as which ad campaign brought you here. This site does not install advertising pixels or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may process technical request information to operate and secure the website.

Where your information goes

Enquiries are stored on our hosting provider, Railway, and are accessible to Maracuya Labs through protected access. If you use an embedded booking calendar, the calendar provider also processes information according to its own privacy notice. The calendar is loaded after you submit an enquiry. Appointment invitations and Google Meet details are managed by the booking provider and Google.

Your choices

Only share information relevant to your enquiry; please do not include sensitive personal information in the form. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your enquiry, or ask us to stop contacting you, using the email above. We keep enquiry information while responding to and discussing your project, and remove it when it is no longer needed, subject to any applicable recordkeeping requirements.
