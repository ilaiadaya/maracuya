Lead with payment implementation: a clear, practical need for startups and small / medium businesses. Use account restrictions as a separate entry message. Commercial model: upfront project fee or revenue share, with scope, eligibility, percentage and duration agreed before work. No invented rates or guaranteed eligibility.
