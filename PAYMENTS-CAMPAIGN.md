## Positioning

Lead with payment implementation: a clear, practical need for startups and small / medium businesses. Use account restrictions as a separate entry message. Offer factual review, appeal preparation and suitable provider options; never guarantee reinstatement, fund recovery or approval. Independent software studio; no claimed Stripe endorsement.

Commercial model: upfront project fee or revenue share, with scope, eligibility, percentage and duration agreed before work. No invented rates or guaranteed eligibility.
